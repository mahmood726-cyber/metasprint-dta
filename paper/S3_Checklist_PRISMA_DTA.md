## Notes

- Item 6 (Protocol): Not applicable as this is a methods/software paper demonstrating an automated platform, not a traditional systematic review with a pre-registered protocol.
- Item 20 (RoB per study): The app generates per-study QUADAS-2 assessments automatically. Individual study-level RoB is not tabulated in the manuscript but is available in the interactive tool.
- All 70 validation topics have PRISMA-DTA flow diagrams generated automatically within the application.
